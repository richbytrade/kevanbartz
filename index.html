01 / ABOUT

A builder who thinks differently.

Kevan Bartz is a scrappy entrepreneur, technology enthusiast, inventor, and investor who operates with the clarity of an abstract thinker and the conviction of a futurist. Based in New York City, he has spent more than a decade founding and scaling companies that create tangible value.

He successfully exited Belmont Knight Associates via acquisition for $25 million. He is also the founder of Lite Garden Inc. and NextGen Business Services — both previous successful exits. He recently launched Lite Garden Capital and raised $174 million for future private equity acquisitions, applying minimalist principles and scrappy execution to institutional capital deployment.

His intellectual property underpins multiple leading sports fantasy and online gambling platforms—quietly powering user experiences for millions while generating significant returns through strategic licensing and partnerships.

Beyond business, Kevan contributes to the scientific community through green energy innovation. His work on wave generation technology and pipeline generators explores new frontiers in sustainable power—turning natural forces and existing infrastructure into clean, efficient energy solutions.

Operating globally • Based in New York City

03 / INNOVATIONS

Green energy.
Real invention.
Future infrastructure.

Wave Generation Technology

Advancing systems that efficiently convert the kinetic energy of ocean and water waves into reliable, clean electricity. Focused on practical deployment, durability in harsh marine environments, and maximizing energy yield for coastal and island communities.

Pipeline Generators

Developing innovative generator technologies integrated with pipeline infrastructure. These systems recover otherwise lost energy from fluid dynamics and pressure differentials, turning existing energy transport networks into distributed power producers.
